## 1. Executive Summary

Proper Place is a consumer-facing online marketplace that connects people looking for private hire venues and accommodation with UK pubs. Pubs list their bookable spaces and rooms on the platform; guests discover, browse, and book directly through proper-place.co.uk or the Proper Place mobile app.

Proper Place earns revenue on every transaction via an 18% booking fee (comprising a 15% platform commission retained by the company and 3% remitted as VAT). After the first 100 host sign-ups, hosts pay an annual listing subscription of £100/year.

The UK pub sector comprises approximately 40,000 venues. A significant proportion — pubs with function rooms, beer gardens, private dining areas, and accommodation — are underserved by existing booking platforms focused on restaurants or hotels. Proper Place addresses this gap with a purpose-built product.

The platform and companion iOS/Android apps are fully built and operational. The founder is currently onboarding hosts through a proprietary CRM tool, with a target of **200 hosts by the end of summer 2026**. Each pub is expected to have an average of 3 bookable spaces and generate 4–5 bookings per week across those spaces from Q3 2026 onwards, at an average booking value of £10–20 per night.

With these assumptions, the business is projected to reach monthly profitability in **Q3 2026** — within months of the first bookings — making this an exceptionally capital-efficient model.

## 3. Problem & Opportunity

### The Problem

**For guests:** Finding a pub with the right private hire space — a function room for 40 people, a beer garden for a summer party, a snug for a team lunch — is fragmented and frustrating. There is no dedicated, searchable marketplace. Most people rely on Google searches, calling venues individually, or using general event platforms that were not built for pubs.

**For pub landlords:** Most pubs have underused spaces during off-peak times. They lack the tools and visibility to attract bookings efficiently. They rely on phone calls, word-of-mouth, and hope. The hospitality sector operates on thin margins; every unfilled hour in a function room is lost revenue.

### The Opportunity

- ~40,000 pubs in the UK (British Beer & Pub Association, 2025)
- An estimated 60% have a bookable space, function room, garden or rooms — approximately **24,000 venues**
- UK private event hire market estimated at **£2.5B+ annually**
- Pub accommodation sector (rooms above pubs, boutique pub stays) growing following lockdown "staycation" shift
- No dominant marketplace specifically for pubs exists; competitors (Hire Space, Tagvenue, Yplanify) focus on corporate events, hotels and general venues — not the community-local-pub segment

## 4. Product & Service

### 4.1 Consumer Platform (proper-place.co.uk)

A web and mobile marketplace where guests can:
- Search pubs by location using an interactive map
- Filter by space type (function room, garden, private dining, accommodation)
- View photos, capacity, pricing and availability
- Book instantly and pay securely through the platform (Stripe payments)
- Leave reviews

### 4.2 Host Dashboard

Pub landlords access a management portal to:
- List spaces with photos, descriptions, pricing and availability calendar
- Receive and manage booking requests
- Communicate with guests via in-platform messaging
- Track earnings and download payouts

### 4.3 Mobile Apps

Native iOS and Android apps (Flutter-based) for both guests and hosts, providing booking management on the go. Available on the App Store and Google Play.

### 4.4 Internal CRM & Sales Tool (Proprietary)

Proper Place has built a bespoke CRM system used internally to prospect and onboard pub hosts. Key features include:
- Map-based lead visualisation with Google Maps satellite view
- Pipeline stage management and lead tracking
- Email outreach directly from the CRM
- Activity logging and visit tracking
- Mobile-accessible for field sales

This is a key operational advantage — the company can run structured, targeted outreach campaigns to pubs at scale without relying on third-party CRM costs.

## 6. Business Model & Revenue Streams

### 6.1 Booking Commission

The primary revenue stream. For every booking completed through the platform:

- **Guest pays:** Booking price + 18% platform fee (15% commission + 3% VAT)
- **Host receives:** Booking price minus Stripe processing (~1.4% + 20p)
- **Proper Place retains:** 15% of booking value (net of 3% VAT remitted to HMRC)

**Example booking:**
> A guest books a pub space at £15/night.
> Guest is charged £15 + £2.70 fee = **£17.70 total**
> Proper Place retains **£2.25** (15% of £15)
> HMRC receives **£0.45** (3% VAT on platform fee)
> Host receives **£15** minus Stripe processing fee (~£0.41)

### 6.2 Host Annual Subscription

From host number 101 onwards, hosts pay **£100/year** to maintain an active listing. The first 100 hosts list for free — an incentive to build the supply side quickly.

This subscription covers:
- Unlimited listing spaces
- Full booking management dashboard
- Priority placement in search results
- Access to analytics and booking insights

## 7. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — Supply First (2026, Months 1–6)

The platform's value depends on having good pub listings. The go-to-market begins on the supply side — onboarding pubs before driving consumer demand.

**Tactics:**
- Direct outreach via the proprietary CRM: field visits, email sequences, phone calls to pub landlords in target areas (London initially, then major UK cities)
- Attend trade events: BII (British Institute of Innkeeping) events, Pub in the Park, local CAMRA events
- Partner with pub groups (JD Wetherspoon independents, regional freehouse groups) to onboard multiple venues quickly
- Incentivise early hosts: first 100 hosts list for free permanently (founding host tier)
- LinkedIn and Instagram content targeting pub landlords — "earn more from your spaces"

**Target: 100 hosts onboarded by end of Q4 2026**

### Phase 2 — Demand Activation (2026 Q3 – 2027)

Once 50+ pubs are listed, begin driving consumer bookings.

**Tactics:**
- SEO: target high-intent queries ("private pub hire London", "book a function room", "pubs with rooms near me")
- Google Ads (search): low CPC in this niche; target event-related searches
- Social media: Instagram and TikTok showing behind-the-scenes pub spaces; user-generated content
- Local press and food/drink editorial (Time Out, Eater London, local regional press)
- Referral programme: guests who refer a booking earn a £10 credit
- Email marketing to event planners, HR teams (work Christmas party), birthday/hen do organisers

### Phase 3 — Scale (2027–2028)

- Expand geographically: London → Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Bristol
- Launch affiliate programme for wedding/event planners
- Integrate with Google Things to Do and Google Hotel Search for accommodation listings
- Explore Series A funding to accelerate sales team and marketing spend

### 10.7 Break-Even Analysis

**Monthly fixed costs:**
- Founder salary: £4,167
- Infrastructure: £350
- **Total: ~£4,517/month**

**Bookings needed to break even:**
> £4,517 ÷ £2.25 per booking = **2,008 bookings/month**

**Hosts needed to break even:**
> 2,008 ÷ 19 bookings/host/month = **~106 active hosts**

With 200 hosts targeted by end of Q3 2026 and the 4–5 bookings/week rate kicking in from that point, the business crosses monthly break-even in **July 2026**.
