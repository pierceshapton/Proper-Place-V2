# The Problem

- Finding a pub with the right private hire space is **fragmented and frustrating**
- No dedicated marketplace exists — people rely on Google, phone calls, or platforms built for hotels and corporate venues
- Pub landlords have **underused spaces** and no efficient way to fill them
- Every unfilled hour in a function room is **lost revenue**

---

# The Solution

## Proper Place

A **purpose-built marketplace** connecting guests with bookable pub spaces across the UK

- Search pubs by location on an **interactive map**
- Filter by space type, capacity, and price
- **Book instantly** and pay securely through the platform
- Available on **web, iOS and Android**

---

# The Market

- **~40,000 pubs** in the UK
- ~60% have a bookable space = **24,000 potential hosts**
- Average 3 bookable spaces per pub
- At 4–5 bookings/week per pub, the **Total Addressable Market** is £82M/year in Gross Booking Value

## No dominant marketplace for pubs exists

Hire Space, Tagvenue and OpenTable focus on corporate events, hotels and restaurants — **not local pubs**

---

# Business Model

## How we make money

| Revenue stream | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| **Booking commission** | 15% of every booking (net of VAT) |
| **Host subscription** | £100/year per pub (after first 100 free founding hosts) |

### Example booking
> Guest books a space at **£15/night**
> Proper Place earns **£2.25**
> Host receives the remainder after Stripe processing

**Simple. Scalable. No upfront cost for early hosts.**
