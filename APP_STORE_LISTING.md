## Common Issues to Avoid

❌ App refers to App Store or Play Store by their exact names  
❌ Incomplete screenshots or watermarks visible  
❌ Pricing information doesn't match actual in-app prices  
❌ Outdated or broken links in description  
❌ Using comparative language (e.g., "best", "leading")  
❌ Requesting reviews or ratings in inappropriate ways  
✅ Always test on actual devices before submission  
✅ Keep privacy/terms links updated and accessible  
✅ Clear, professional description and screenshots  
✅ Include key features and benefits prominently
